Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SmartStudy.

Is SmartStudy free?
Yes! SmartStudy offers a free plan with all core features. We also offer premium plans for advanced analytics, collaboration, and unlimited storage.
Can I use SmartStudy on mobile?
Absolutely! SmartStudy is fully responsive and works great on all devices. You can even install it as a progressive web app for offline access.
How is my data stored and protected?
We use bank-level encryption (AES-256) to protect your data. All data is stored in secure, distributed servers and you retain complete control over your information.
Can I export my data?
Yes, you can export all your data in multiple formats (CSV, PDF, JSON) at any time. Your data is always yours.
Do you offer student discounts?
Yes! Students can get 50% off premium plans with a valid .edu email address. We believe education should be affordable.
